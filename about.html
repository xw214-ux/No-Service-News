About

NO SERVICE is an independent journal and radio thread for deep bass music: sound-system weight · meditative pressure · underdog signal.

The site stays close to records with sub-pressure, restraint, dub technique, and a sense of space. Some weeks that means a pile of leads. Some weeks it means one record worth replaying until the room changes shape.

Everything here is meant to be used: listen in the deck, jump back to the original source, and follow the archive as it takes form. When the plates drop, support the artists on their own pages.

Start here

New? Here is the lane in plain terms. No Service tracks deep, weighty, meditative 140 and dubstep — the kind of low-end built for a sound system, not a festival drop. Think dread, patience, negative space, and dub technique over hooks and hype. Alongside it: rolling minimal jungle and dark halftime when the sound design earns it.

A few ways in:

  • Journal — field notes and selections in the No Service voice.
  • Radio — selections in show format, pressure-first listening order.
  • Submit — sending a record? Read the brief first.

The bar is restraint, not volume. If a tune earns the room going quiet, it belongs here.
