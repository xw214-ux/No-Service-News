Submit to the Desk

No Service is a listening desk for sound-system weight, meditative pressure, underdog signal, and scenes that are doing real work before the algorithm notices.

What helps

Send track links, label pages, Bandcamp drops, SoundCloud premieres, radio archives, local context, artwork credits, and release notes. The best submissions include why it matters and where it sits: 140, dubstep, grime pressure, bass mutations, left-field club, radio-only dubs, or a local scene thread.

What happens next

Submissions go into the desk queue for Clu breadth scans, Coda deep-cut passes, and Signal editorial synthesis. A submission is not a promise of coverage, playlisting, radio use, licensing, or upload rights.

Send

For now, use the project inbox or DM channel Xanz already uses for No Service leads. Keep rights notes explicit: official stream, promo permission, public embed only, or private listening reference.
